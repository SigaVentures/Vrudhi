About Us

Made for you, stitch by stitch.

Vrudhi Designer Boutique & Tailoring is a custom designer studio on Villianur Main Road, Reddiarpalayam, specialising in bespoke bridal blouses and lehenga tailoring with a focus on precision fitting.

Bring us your saree, your fabric or simply an idea. From intricate Aari and zardozi handwork to mother–daughter and family combo outfits, kidswear and photoshoot dresses, every piece is customised to your style — with 15+ years of experience behind every stitch.

  • Perfect FitMeasured and stitched to you.
  • Premium FinishingNeat, elegant detailing throughout.
  • Aari HandworkZari, beads, stones and zardozi.
